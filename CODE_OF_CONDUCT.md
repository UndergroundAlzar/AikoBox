# 社区行为准则 / Community Code of Conduct

## 中文

AikoBox 社区欢迎不同经验、背景和观点的参与者。参与 Issue、PR、评审、讨论或其他项目空间时，请保持尊重、耐心和专业。

我们鼓励：

- 针对代码和事实讨论，不攻击个人；
- 清楚说明依据、风险和不确定性，愿意修正错误；
- 尊重他人的时间、隐私、署名和劳动成果；
- 帮助新参与者理解上下文，并接受善意的技术分歧。

我们不接受：

- 骚扰、歧视、侮辱、威胁、跟踪或煽动敌意；
- 未经同意公开身份、联系方式、凭据、订阅、日志或其他私人信息；
- 垃圾信息、恶意干扰、冒充、蓄意误导或反复无视维护者边界；
- 以任何形式施压他人降低安全、隐私或许可证要求。

如遇不当行为，请通过 [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) 所述的私密渠道联系维护者，并仅提供处理事件所需的信息。维护者会尽量保密、公平核实，并可采取提醒、限制互动、删除内容、临时或永久禁止参与等措施。紧急人身危险应联系所在地的紧急服务机构。

本准则适用于项目仓库、官方讨论空间，以及代表 AikoBox 参与的公开活动。维护者也受同样标准约束。

## English

The AikoBox community welcomes people with different levels of experience, backgrounds, and viewpoints. Be respectful, patient, and professional in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.

We encourage participants to:

- discuss code and evidence without attacking people;
- explain reasoning, risks, and uncertainty, and correct mistakes openly;
- respect others' time, privacy, attribution, and work;
- help newcomers gain context and treat technical disagreement in good faith.

We do not accept:

- harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, stalking, or incitement;
- exposing identities, contact details, credentials, subscriptions, logs, or private information without consent;
- spam, deliberate disruption, impersonation, deception, or repeated disregard of maintainer boundaries;
- pressure to weaken security, privacy, or licensing requirements.

Report misconduct privately through the channel described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), sharing only what is necessary to handle the incident. Maintainers will aim for confidentiality and a fair review, and may issue a warning, limit interaction, remove content, or impose a temporary or permanent ban. Contact local emergency services if anyone faces immediate physical danger.

This code applies in the repository, official project spaces, and public activities where someone represents AikoBox. Maintainers are held to the same standard.
